🔢 Algebraic Expressions

Building blocks of higher math

An algebraic expression contains numbers, variables & operations.

Parts:

  • Variable — letter (x, y, a)
  • Coefficient — number with variable (5 in 5x)
  • Constant — fixed number (+7 in 3x+7)

Example: 3x² − 5y + 8

Lesson 2 of 5 • Day 2

📐 Laws of Exponents

Shortcuts for powers

Product Rule

aᵐ × aⁿ = aᵐ⁺ⁿ

Same base → ADD exponents

Quotient Rule

aᵐ ÷ aⁿ = aᵐ⁻ⁿ

Same base → SUBTRACT exponents

Power Rule

(aᵐ)ⁿ = aᵐ×ⁿ

Power of power → MULTIPLY exponents

Lesson 3 of 5 • Day 3

📏 Linear Equations

Finding the unknown

Equation has = — both sides must balance!

Steps:

  1. Simplify
  2. Isolate variable
  3. Do same to BOTH sides
  4. Check answer

Example: 2x + 5 = 15 → 2x = 10 → x = 5 ✅

Lesson 4 of 5 • Day 4

📐 Pythagorean Theorem

Right triangles only!

Formula: a² + b² = c²

  • a, b = legs (shorter sides)
  • c = hypotenuse (longest side)

Example: 3² + 4² = 9 + 16 = 25 → c = 5 ✅

Lesson 5 of 5 • Day 5

📊 Functions

Each input → ONE output

Terms:

  • Domain = all x-values (inputs)
  • Range = all y-values (outputs)
  • f(x) = "f of x" = same as y

f(x) = 2x + 3 → if x=2, then f(2)=7 ✅

Lesson 1 of 5 • Day 1

⚛️ Matter

Mass + Volume = Matter

Physical vs Chemical:

  • Physical → same substance, different look (melting, cutting)
  • Chemical → NEW substance formed (burning, rusting)

3 States:

  • 🔹 Solid — fixed shape & volume
  • 🔹 Liquid — fixed volume, takes container shape
  • 🔹 Gas — no fixed shape/volume

Lesson 2 of 5 • Day 2

💨 Elements & Compounds

Pure vs Mixed

Pure Substances:

  • Element — one type of atom only (O, C, Fe)
  • Compound — 2+ elements bonded (H₂O, CO₂)

Mixtures:

  • Homogeneous — uniform throughout (salt water)
  • Heterogeneous — visible different parts (sand + water)

Lesson 3 of 5 • Day 3

⚡ Force & Motion

Newton's 3 Laws

  • 1st — Inertia: things keep doing what they're doing until forced to change
  • 2nd — F = ma: Force = mass × acceleration
  • 3rd — Action-Reaction: every action has equal opposite reaction

Speed = distance ÷ time

🔋 Energy

Never created — only transformed

Two Main Types:

  • Potential — stored (position, chemical)
  • Kinetic — motion (faster = more KE)

Examples: Sun → solar panel → electricity → light 💡

📝 Parts of Speech

8 building blocks

  • Noun — person/place/thing → teacher, Manila
  • Pronoun — replaces noun → I, you, they
  • Verb — action/state → run, is
  • Adjective — describes noun → beautiful, red
  • Adverb — describes verb → quickly, very
  • Preposition — position → in, on, under
  • Conjunction — connects → and, but, because
  • Interjection — emotion → Wow!

Subject + Predicate

4 Types:

  • Declarative — statement .
  • Interrogative — question ?
  • Imperative — command . / !
  • Exclamatory — emotion !

⏰ Verb Tenses

Past • Present • Future

  • Present — happens now → walks
  • Past — already done → walked
  • Future — will happen → will walk
  • Progressive — ongoing → am walking, was walking
